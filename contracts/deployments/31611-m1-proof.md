## Reproducibility Note

Foundry's revm cannot execute Mezo's native precompiles (`0x7B7c...0015` BTC price feed, `0x7B7c...0001` MEZO token). `forge script` therefore cannot simulate or broadcast this flow even when forking live 31611. The proof above was executed with `cast send` / `cast call` against the live RPC. The Solidity script `script/VerifyMilestone1OnChain.s.sol` is the canonical sequence for environments that implement the precompiles.

A follow-up `injectWeeklyFeeYield(5e18)` from the deployer ([`0x5be08cbda3ac530608a04f7030057889ea4608988f68c29c1f5047e710233d97`](https://explorer.test.mezo.org/tx/0x5be08cbda3ac530608a04f7030057889ea4608988f68c29c1f5047e710233d97), block `16382307`) returned `status = 0` because the one-week injection cooldown was active. It is excluded from the 5/5 count and did not change state.
